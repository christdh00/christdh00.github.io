01 / MUSICAL JOURNEY

Eleven years of music education.

From the foundations of musicianship to an intensive pre-conservatory programme at Sekolah Yayasan Pendidikan Musik (YPM).

YPM · ELEMENTARY LEVEL ♪

The foundations

Alongside classical piano lessons, I studied music theory, solfège, choir, and recorder—building the listening, reading, and ensemble skills that shaped my later studies.

YPM · PRE-CONSERVATORY ♫

Pre-conservatory studies

My studies expanded to advanced theory and solfège, music history, psychology, pedagogy, and counterpoint, alongside more demanding piano repertoire.

2024 · FINAL EXAMINATION ♬

The final recital

I completed my YPM studies with a six-work recital programme, including a compulsory piece announced only about six weeks before the examination.
